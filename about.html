Our Story

Why We Started

ExperienceLink was founded with a simple idea: every student deserves the opportunity to gain real-world experience before graduation.

"We noticed that many talented students struggle to land their first internship because most positions require prior experience."

At the same time, companies have countless small projects that could benefit from fresh perspectives and eager talent. We saw an opportunity to create something that would benefit everyone.

ExperienceLink bridges this gap by creating a platform where students can work on real projects, build portfolios, and connect with companies - all while still in school. We're not just a job board; we're a launchpad for the next generation of professionals.
